# Eclipse Apoapsis Sponsorship Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Eclipse Apoapsis accepts financial sponsorship to support the sustainability of the project.
This policy applies to the whole Eclipse Apoapsis project, including all repositories of the [eclipse-apoapsis](https://github.com/eclipse-apoapsis) GitHub organization.
It supplements, and does not replace, the policies and processes of the Eclipse Foundation, which take precedence in case of conflict.

This project accepts sponsorship through Open Collective, fiscally hosted by Open Collective Europe.
Funds support project-related activities according to the project's published sponsorship and spending policy.
Sponsorship does not grant technical influence, governance rights, committer status, review privileges, or Eclipse Foundation membership benefits.
Eclipse Foundation does not receive, hold, invoice, approve, or disburse sponsorship funds or funds contributed through this sponsorship model.

## 2. Fiscal Host

All funds are held by the fiscal host [Open Source Europe](https://opencollective.com/europe) (formerly and legally Open Collective Europe ASBL/vzw) in the project's collective at [opencollective.com/eclipse-apoapsis](https://opencollective.com/eclipse-apoapsis).
Contributions made through the project's GitHub Sponsors profile go to the same collective.
All expenses must also comply with the [expense rules of the fiscal host](https://www.opensourceeurope.org/docs/how-it-works/expenses/).

## 3. Sponsorship Is a Donation

Sponsorship is a donation style contribution.
No goods or services are expected in return.

Sponsors do not receive technical decision-making authority, committer status, preferential treatment for issues, reviews, roadmap, or releases, or any other governance rights.
They follow the same governance rules as every other member of the community.
The project leads may decline or refund a sponsorship.

## 4. Funding Purpose

Funds are used for activities that benefit the project, such as:

* Project infrastructure and external services.
* Development-related vendors or contractors.
* Travel of contributors to represent the project.
* Testing, quality, and release support.
* Design, UX, translation, or marketing support.
* Sponsorship of individual committers or companies for work on the project.

## 5. Eligible Recipients

* Anyone can be paid for an approved expense, including committers, other contributors, vendors, and companies.
* New committers are eligible immediately.
* Committers who leave the project are still paid for expenses that were approved before they left.
* Funds do not pay for work that is already paid for by another source, such as an employer.

## 6. Approval and Payout

* Expenses must be approved by the project leads in a public [GitHub discussion](https://github.com/eclipse-apoapsis/ort-server/discussions) before money is spent or work is started.
* After that, the expense is submitted on Open Collective with an invoice or receipt and a link to the GitHub discussion, approved there by a project lead, and paid out by the fiscal host.
* Nobody approves their own expense.
  If no project lead without a conflict of interest is available, the project committers decide by simple majority.

## 7. Sponsor Recognition

* Sponsors are listed on the Open Collective and GitHub Sponsors pages.
* Sponsors may be recognized by a logo in the project's README according to their sponsorship tier, as far as permitted by the [Eclipse Foundation branding rules](https://www.eclipse.org/projects/handbook/#company-logos).
* Sponsors and sponsored individuals or companies are listed neutrally: grouped by tier where applicable, and alphabetically within a group.
* Sponsor recognition does not imply endorsement by the Eclipse Foundation.

## 8. Unused Funds

Unused funds carry forward and remain available for the project.
If the project is archived or terminated, the project leads transfer the remaining funds to other open source projects or initiatives, never to individuals.

## 9. Transparency and Changes

* All contributions and expenses are publicly visible on Open Collective.
* Changes to this policy require the agreement of all project leads.
  The Eclipse Foundation is notified of major changes to the sponsorship model.
